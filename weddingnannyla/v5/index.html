Personalized care and group sizes

Wedding Nanny LA prioritizes attentive, personalized care by keeping thoughtfully planned group sizes, based on the children's ages, their developmental needs and what the event requires. For smaller events, one caregiver may look after a limited group of children. Larger groups, and events with younger children, may need more caregivers, so every child is properly supervised and comfortable.

    Questions couples and parents ask

    Anything else, call or text 818-282-0492.

    How is the price worked out?

    We charge per caregiver, per hour, not per child. Our proposed starting rate is $50 per caregiver, per hour. The number of caregivers depends on how many children are coming, their ages and the level of staffing your event needs. The calculator on this page gives a rough estimate.

    Is there a minimum booking?

    Yes. Weddings have a 4-hour minimum and private events have a 3-hour minimum. Additional hours are billed at the hourly rate per caregiver.

    Does sending an inquiry reserve my date?

    No. We review your inquiry, confirm availability and send a custom proposal. Your booking is confirmed once the agreement is signed and the deposit is received.

    How do the deposit and balance work?

    A 30% non-refundable booking retainer secures your date, subject to the final contract and its cancellation terms. The remaining balance is due 7 days before the event.

    Where do you work?

    Los Angeles, the San Fernando Valley, Calabasas, Malibu, Santa Clarita, Ventura County and Orange County, plus other surrounding areas on request. Travel fees may apply outside our standard Los Angeles service area. Destination weddings and out-of-area events are considered case by case, depending on the location, staffing, travel and event details.

    What if the party runs late?

    No problem. Additional hours are billed at the hourly rate per caregiver, so the kids are covered until the parents are ready.

    How do parents check their kids in?

    Before the event, each parent fills in a short registration form for each child: full name and age, parent contact details, an emergency contact, allergies and dietary restrictions, medical or care instructions, and who's authorized to pick them up.

    What will the kids do?

    Supervised arts and crafts, structured play, games and a quiet corner, set up in a dedicated space at your venue and planned around the event schedule.
