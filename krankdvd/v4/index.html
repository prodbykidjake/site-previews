How booking works

Four steps from request to record. Nothing is charged until David approves, and rooms without a set rate get their price before you pay.

  1. Request

    Pick a studio, a room, a date and how many hours. It goes straight to David by text and email.

  2. Approve

    He checks the room and his calendar, then approves it or suggests another time.

  3. Pay

    Card through Stripe, Zelle or Cash App. Nothing is charged before he approves.

  4. Record

    Your confirmation arrives with the studio address. Bring the song.

Fair questions

How do I book a session?

Pick a room on this page and fill out the short form. David gets your request by text and email. Once he approves it, you'll get a payment link, and you're booked when payment comes through.

Is my time held as soon as I send a request?

Not yet. Sending a request tells David what you want. Your time is held once he approves it and you've paid.

How do I pay?

After David approves your request, you'll get a link to pay by card through Stripe. You can also pay by Zelle or Cash App ($chnxbeatsllc). Put your booking ID in the payment note so David can match it to your session.

Why do some rooms say ask for rate?

The Brick and the Vault at Weddington Studios don't have a set hourly rate yet. Send a request anyway and David will reply with the price before you pay anything.

What's the special budget invoice?

Working with a set budget or booking a bigger project at Studio Lyfe or Weddington Studios? Ask for a quote, tell David what you have to spend, and he'll send you a custom price.

What's a lease versus a custom beat?

A lease ($60) gets you a beat David has already made. A custom beat is made from scratch for your song: $125 with a one-week turnaround, or $200 for the same day.

What should I bring to the session?

Your lyrics, any beats or stems as WAV files, and a couple of reference tracks for the sound you're going for.
