Meet David

David, known as KrankDVD, records artists, mixes their records and makes beats from scratch in eight rooms across North Hollywood.

David is a producer and engineer based in North Hollywood. He works out of Studio Lyfe, Weddington Studios and Boss Gang Studio, so you can pick the room, the mic and the budget that fit your session.

Every request goes straight to him by text and email. He approves it, sends a payment link, and your time is held once you've paid.

How booking works

  1. Step 1

    Pick a room

    Eight rooms in three North Hollywood studios, from $65 an hour. Each one lists its rate and its gear above.

  2. Step 2

    Send a request

    Choose a date, a start time and how many hours. It goes straight to David by text and email.

  3. Step 3

    David approves

    He checks the room and his calendar, then approves it or suggests another time. Rooms without a set rate get their price here.

  4. Step 4

    Pay to lock it in

    Card through Stripe, Zelle or Cash App. Nothing is charged before he approves.

  5. Step 5

    You're booked

    Your confirmation arrives by email with the studio address.

  6. Step 6

    Press record

    Bring your lyrics, any beats or stems as WAV files, and a couple of reference tracks.

Beats and session rates

Studio time is booked by the hour at the room you pick. Beats are a flat price.

  1. Recording · from $65 an hour

    David engineers your session from setup to bounce: vocals, instruments, full songs.

  2. Mixing · from $65 an hour

    Mix the record you just tracked, or bring stems from somewhere else and mix them with David.

  3. Custom beat · $125, one week

    Made from scratch for your song. Send the vibe, the tempo and a few references.

  4. Same-day beat · $200

    The same custom beat, delivered the day you order it.

  5. Beat lease · $60

    A beat David has already made, leased for your song.

Nothing is charged until David approves your request.

Before you book

Anything not answered here, text David at (213) 642-6551.

How do I book a session?

Pick a room on this page and fill out the short form. David gets your request by text and email. Once he approves it, you'll get a payment link, and you're booked when payment comes through.

Is my time held as soon as I send a request?

Not yet. Sending a request tells David what you want. Your time is held once he approves it and you've paid.

How do I pay?

After David approves your request, you'll get a link to pay by card through Stripe. You can also pay by Zelle or Cash App ($chnxbeatsllc). Put your booking ID in the payment note so David can match it to your session.

Why do some rooms say ask for rate?

The Brick and the Vault at Weddington Studios don't have a set hourly rate yet. Send a request anyway and David will reply with the price before you pay anything.

What's the special budget invoice?

Working with a set budget or booking a bigger project at Studio Lyfe or Weddington Studios? Ask for a quote, tell David what you have to spend, and he'll send you a custom price.

What's a lease versus a custom beat?

A lease ($60) gets you a beat David has already made. A custom beat is made from scratch for your song: $125 with a one-week turnaround, or $200 for the same day.

What should I bring to the session?

Your lyrics, any beats or stems as WAV files, and a couple of reference tracks for the sound you're going for.
