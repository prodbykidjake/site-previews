FAQ

Something else on your mind? Text David at (213) 642-6551.

How do I book a session?

Pick a studio and room above and fill out the short form. David gets your request by text and email. Once he approves it, you'll get a payment link, and you're booked when payment comes through.

Is my time held as soon as I send a request?

Not yet. Sending a request just tells David what you want. Your time is held once he approves it and you've paid.

How do I pay?

After David approves your request, you'll get a link to pay by card through Stripe. You can also pay by Zelle or Cash App ($chnxbeatsllc). Put your booking ID in the payment note so David can match it to your session.

What's the special budget invoice?

Working with a set budget or booking a bigger project? Choose "Special budget" on any studio, tell David what you have to spend, and he'll send you a custom quote.

What's the difference between the A, B and C rooms?

They're different rooms at the same studio, priced by size and gear. Open "Gear in this studio" under each studio to see what's there, or text David if you're not sure which room fits your session.

What's a lease versus a custom beat?

A lease ($60) gets you a beat David has already made. A custom beat is made from scratch for your song: $125 with a one-week turnaround, or $200 for the same day.

What should I bring to the session?

Your lyrics, any beats or stems as WAV files, and a couple of reference tracks for the sound you're going for.
